---
name: clip-gameplay
description: Cut a gameplay video or stream into vertical Shorts / Reels / TikToks. Finds the best action moments (fights, clutches, wins, fails) from sound, motion and the frames, shows a scored table for approval, then renders 1080x1920 clips with a hook headline, facecam split or full-screen framing, and captions when someone talks. Use when the user runs /clip-gameplay, or gives a YouTube link or video file of a game and wants shorts, highlights or best moments.
---

**Mode is fixed: gameplay.** The user chose this command, so do not work out
what kind of video it is. Read `references/gameplay.md` before step 3 and
follow it. Everything else below applies as written.
