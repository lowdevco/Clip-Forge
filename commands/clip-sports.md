---
name: clip-sports
description: Cut sports footage or a match broadcast into vertical Shorts / Reels / TikToks. Finds goals, wickets, points, saves and finishes from crowd and commentator noise and from scoreboard changes, shows a scored table for approval, then renders edited 1080x1920 clips with a headline, punch-in and slow-motion on the key moment, and the real score as a callout. Use when the user runs /clip-sports, or gives a YouTube link or video file of a match, game or race and wants highlights, shorts or best moments.
---

**Mode is fixed: sports.** The user chose this command, so do not work out
what kind of video it is. Read `references/sports.md` before step 3 and
follow it. Everything else below applies as written.
