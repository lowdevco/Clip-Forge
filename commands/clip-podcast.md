---
name: clip-podcast
description: Cut a podcast, interview or talking-head video into vertical Shorts / Reels / TikToks. Finds the strongest self-contained passages from the transcript, shows a scored table for approval, then renders 1080x1920 clips cropped onto the speaker with word-by-word captions (English and Malayalam), a hook headline and silences trimmed. Use when the user runs /clip-podcast, or gives a YouTube link or video file of a podcast or interview and wants shorts, clips or viral parts.
---

**Mode is fixed: podcast.** The user chose this command, so do not work out
what kind of video it is. Read `references/podcast.md` before step 3 and
follow it. Everything else below applies as written.
