---
name: clip-video
description: Cut any long video (vlog, tutorial, review, event, sports, travel) into vertical Shorts / Reels / TikToks. Finds the best moments from both the transcript and the pictures, shows a scored table for approval, then renders 1080x1920 clips with captions, a hook headline and loudness-normalised audio. Use when the user runs /clip-video, or gives a YouTube link or video file that is neither a podcast nor gameplay and wants shorts, clips or highlights.
---

**Mode is fixed: video (general).** The user chose this command, so do not
work out what kind of video it is. Read `references/video.md` before step 3
and follow it. Everything else below applies as written.
